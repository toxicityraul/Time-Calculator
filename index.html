Rules used by this calculator

  • UPT accrues at 5 minutes per hour actually worked.
  • The single-shift prediction prorates that rate by minutes worked and rounds down to a whole minute.
  • UPT balance is capped at 80 hours.
  • UPT attendance gaps are charged in 15-minute blocks.
  • Only the balance rounded down to a full 15-minute block is available when planning a maximum early leave.
  • Flexible PTO and Standard PTO can be combined and used by the minute.
  • Unpaid lunch time is excluded from work and time-off coverage.
This is an unofficial planning tool. Your employer's timekeeping system is the final source of truth.
